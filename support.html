Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add an accountability partner?

Go to the Partners tab and send an invite by Messages, QR code, or link. Your partner opens the invite, installs HabitFlame if needed, and enters the code you shared. Once both sides connect, you can share selected habits and send nudges.

I have an invite code. Where do I enter it?

Open HabitFlame, go to the Partners tab, tap Enter Code, and type the six character code from your invite. Codes expire after seven days, so if yours no longer works, ask your partner to send a new invite.

How do I create a new habit?

Tap the plus button on the Today tab. Fill in your habit details, choose an icon and color, set your schedule, and save.

What is a Duo Quest?

A Duo Quest is a shared goal you and your accountability partner work toward together. You both make progress toward the same target, which makes staying consistent more fun and harder to quietly abandon.

How does Apple Health auto-complete work?

For habits tied to activity, such as steps or workouts, you can let HabitFlame read the relevant Apple Health data and mark the habit complete automatically. You choose exactly what to authorize, and you can revoke access at any time in the Health app or iOS Settings.

What happens if I miss a day?

Your streak will reset, but every day is a fresh start. Your history and stats are preserved, so you can always see your overall progress.

Can I use HabitFlame on multiple devices?

Yes. With iCloud sync enabled, your habits stay in sync across your iPhone and iPad automatically.

How do I restore a purchase?

Go to Settings, then the upgrade section, and choose Restore Purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.
