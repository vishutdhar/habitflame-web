Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

Introduction

HabitFlame ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our iOS application.

Information We Collect

We do not collect your personal information for our own use. HabitFlame is designed with privacy in mind:

  • Your habit data is stored locally on your device.
  • We do not operate servers that collect or store your habit history.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Accountability Partners

If you choose to connect with an accountability partner, some information is shared to make that feature work:

  • The habits you explicitly choose to share, along with their completion status and streaks, are visible to the partner you connect with.
  • Connecting requires exchanging an invite code or link. This is used only to pair your devices and is not used to identify you beyond the partnership.
  • Partner notifications and nudges are delivered through Apple Push Notification service. Delivery tokens are used solely to route these messages between partners.
  • You can stop sharing at any time by removing a partner or unsharing a habit in the app.

Apple Health

If you enable Apple Health integration, HabitFlame reads only the specific health data you authorize (for example, steps or workouts) to automatically complete matching habits. This data is read on your device, is not sent to us, and you can revoke access at any time in the Health app or iOS Settings.

iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync, your habit data is stored in your personal iCloud account. This data is:

  • Encrypted and protected by Apple.
  • Only accessible by you through your Apple ID.
  • Not accessible by the HabitFlame developer.

Data Storage

Your habit data, including habit names, completion history, and streaks, is stored:

  • Locally on your device using Apple's secure storage.
  • Optionally in your personal iCloud account, if you enable sync.

Third-Party Services

HabitFlame uses Apple services (Push Notifications, iCloud, and HealthKit) to provide its features. It does not integrate third-party advertising networks or sell data to third parties.

Children's Privacy

HabitFlame does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
