# Lessons

One line each: the mistake, the rule that prevents it, the date. Read before the first change in a session.

- 2026-11-02: Six pages, a shared FAQ answer, the terms and the privacy policy said a free pairing can choose which habits a partner follows; in the app that is Premium only. Every claim about a feature's tier is checked against the app source before it is written, and the gate pins the tier in the same sentence.
- 2026-11-02: Copy said a completion reaches the partner "the moment you do it" without saying it applies to completions made in the app for today (widget completions and backfills send no alert). A qualified claim is written in full every time, never shortened for rhythm.
- 2026-11-02: The New Year plan promised milestones by calendar week for any schedule; a weekly target's streak advances by whole weeks and can step past a milestone. A plan or timeline names the schedule it assumes.
- 2026-11-02: The structured data description was verified against itself (the gate read the expected value from the same config). A gate pins a claim word for word from a source outside the thing it checks.
- 2026-11-02: A gate rule written at paragraph level let three misleading sentences pass because the qualifier came later in the paragraph. Copy rules are sentence level.
- 2026-11-02: measure.js skipped only localhost and 127.0.0.1, so private network copies would have sent production events. Any host that is not public is excluded, listed by range, with a test per range.
